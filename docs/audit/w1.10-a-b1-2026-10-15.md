## Limitations and next task

These are **synthetic WAN mutations in memory**, not real Linux route recovery. No real NIC, router, modem, measured bandwidth, physical powercut, storage controller, PKI/mTLS, watchdog/OTA, physical performance/thermals, energy or BOM/TCO was tested. OS fsync cannot certify media durability. A route command might complete after timeout and independently mutate kernel state; deadlines, cancellation semantics and boot reconciliation are W1.10.B2.
