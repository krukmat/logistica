## Missing physical requirements / risks

The C1 fixture is **not** a real Linux kernel route driver, no trusted device identity store and **no actual on-device provisioning**. Lab boot intent is unsigned, mutable on disk and lacks an authenticated anti-rollback design. `AbortSignal` is advisory; old system processes may still modify routes after reboot. No kernel-grade exclusive writer, root-owned service, secure boot, mTLS key generation, certificate rotation, OTA installation/rollback, watchdog qualification, real flash/SSD powercut validation or hardware cost/power/thermal/throughput metrics. Neither PID lock nor synthetic route reconciliation is sufficient for production.

**Closure:** C1 **CLOSED for contract + laboratory rehearsal only**. `device` remains physically BLOCKED and cannot be enabled based on this report or operator supplied claims. Continue with C2 hardware evaluation and physical qualification; require separate design approval before implementing privileged device drivers.
