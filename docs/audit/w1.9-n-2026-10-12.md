## W1.9.N evidence and acceptance

**N1 — Backpressure, compaction and retry (PASS in lab):**
- A bounded file-journal fixture accepts the initial primary, failover and all-WAN-offline transition events. The next transition exceeds the regular quota, returns `ack=false`, and the runtime reports `DEGRADED`, while Connectivity still selects the recovered WAN.
- A durable `CLOUD_ACK` of the already-received O2 event allows safe compaction into a digest-preserving reference. A later sample retries the previously volatile transition and obtains an actual host-OS `fsync` ACK; reopening the journal confirms the record and the critical offline O1 evidence.
- **No false ACK** while space was exhausted. The test fixture quota was tuned to trigger this boundary deterministically, rather than weakening the oracle.

**N2 — Reopen and session identity (PASS in lab):** after closing the runtime, reopening with a distinct session epoch preserves previous ACKed records and generates a new event ID for subsequent observations. The session epoch is **not a device identity**; deduplication/reconciliation across an actual appliance reboot requires stable device identity and cloud protocol definition.

**N3 — Negative/limits (PASS in lab):** a deliberately limited, volatile retry queue reports dropped evidence honestly when flooded; `LOCAL_ONLY` and local routing continue, with no false `fsync` or physical validation claim. A route-application failure still prevents an incorrect journal claim.

## Limitations

- The retry queue lives in RAM and is intentionally bounded: it is **not durable**, and an event may be lost if the process crashes before space is recovered.
- The P1 compacted-id tombstone set is itself bounded by disk capacity; infinite offline records and unbounded event-ID history are not guaranteed.
- `fsync` and process-reopen tests do not reproduce real power removal, SSD controller write-cache behavior, vibration, thermal effects, hardware NIC/ISP failover, or real p95 latency.
- Workload Runtime, Trust+Recovery bootstrap, signed OTA, credentials and physical resource enforcement remain unimplemented or unvalidated in this integrated slice.
