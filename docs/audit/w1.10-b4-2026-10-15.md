Mac parity requires `npm run traces:check -- --output /tmp/pef-golden-mac.json` on the Mac and `npm run traces:check -- --compare /absolute/pef-golden-traces-linux.json` against the trusted extracted Actions artifact. Linux pass does not prove Mac pass, and matching normalized behavior on Mac cannot certify kernel/device drivers.
