- `src/emulator/lab/property-smoke.js` and Control Panel `propertySmoke` action exercise the real standalone Edge. The dashboard displays structured checks; it does not create an unrelated Property simulator.

## Scope and outstanding risks

- `NODE_PROCESS_ONLY` is **not** Linux sandbox; `actualLimitsEnforced=false` for Property. Linux isolated process driver remains receiver-focused until separately extended and validated.
- Lab journal fsync is host-OS fsync only; no physical power-loss, long offline endurance/72h quota, storage fault-injection certification or device testing.
- WAN links are synthetic and Cloud is a same-host mock; no secure hosted Cloud Lite, PKI, signed provisioning, multi-WAN physical ports or real guest/PMS integrations.
- Business need, installer complexity, support economics and customer demand are unvalidated. No spend or procurement authorized.

**Decision:** close M4.1.1–M4.1.3 **only in software lab**. Continue M4.2 Host as **CONFIG/MODULE/RESOURCE** overlay on the existing Core; validate cross-overlay commonality and stress in M4.3 before M4.4 full release evidence.
