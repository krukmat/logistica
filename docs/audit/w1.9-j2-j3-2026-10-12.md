## Residual risks and acceptance boundary

1. Tombstone refs consume bounded P1 capacity indefinitely. Deleting old refs requires explicit deduplication-horizon/retention policy and cloud-side coordination: **OPEN**. No infinite offline backlog guarantee.
2. Filesystem compaction assumes single writer; no per-process or OS lock and no multi-host coordination. Directory entry/rename atomicity and `fsync` on hosted Ubuntu do not prove physical power-loss semantics.
3. `testHook` is a lab-only interrupt injector; a device adapter must not expose this to untrusted workload inputs.
4. Need a **working integrated Core runtime** (route policy + persistent journal + recovery) instead of only independent emulator ports, before W1.8 hardware comparison.

**J2 DoD:** IMPLEMENTED + TESTED in hosted Node/Linux lab, not physically VALIDATED. **J3 DoD:** evidence scope and outstanding gates documented. **Recommended next engineering block:** W1.9.L — connect controller transitions to durable journal events and introduce runtime-level negative tests before device selection.
