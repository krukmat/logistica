## Product engineering implication: resource envelope still open

The current W1.5 Base planning scenario specifies a **4 GiB PoC fixture**, ~1,760 MiB modeled allowances and a **72h offline journal at 2 events/s** (potentially 0.49–1.98 GiB raw records at 1–4 KiB/record). **This 1 GiB VM proof is for the minimal Core workload only** and must not silently supersede those requirements. Two thousand short receipts are far smaller than that backlog scenario.

Current `src/adapters/os-file-journal.js` loads the whole file with `fs.readFileSync(file)` and populates `Map` indices during reopen, an implementation likely to scale memory usage with journal length. A bounded test on 1 GiB is necessary before expanding the journal capacity or accepting 72h offline operation.
